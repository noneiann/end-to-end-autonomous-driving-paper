\subsection{Per-Lap Tracking Consistency}
\label{sec:per_lap_consistency}
Because each evaluation run is a continuous, multi-lap circuit, tracking
consistency is reported per \emph{lap} rather than over arbitrary fixed-length
frame windows. A lap is one complete circuit of the track, so a per-lap window
aligns each consistency sample with a physically meaningful unit of the course
instead of an $8.3$~s slice that spans a variable fraction of a lap. The
$45^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ runs each comprise 10 laps (lap periods of
$\approx 4.9$~s and $\approx 2.9$~s respectively); the $180^\circ$ endurance run
comprises 8 complete laps, its two $180^\circ$ turns per lap yielding a lap
period of $\approx 26$~s. Mirroring the earlier policy of discarding partial
trailing windows, the $180^\circ$ run's final partial lap ($14.3$~s) is excluded
so that only complete laps contribute. Per-lap RMSE summaries are given in
Table~\ref{tab:per_lap_consistency}, with the lap-by-lap values in
Table~\ref{tab:per_lap_detail}.

The lap-resolved view exposes structure that the run-level averages obscure.
The $45^\circ$ run shows an initial settling transient: the vehicle begins
displaced from the path (laps 1--2, RMSE $4.56$ and $3.59$~px) and converges
within two laps to tight tracking (laps 3--9 all below $1.8$~px), with a single
elevated final lap. Both the $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ runs exhibit a
mid-run excursion followed by recovery rather than monotonic drift: error peaks
near the middle of each run ($90^\circ$ laps 5--6, up to $7.02$~px;
$180^\circ$ laps 5--6, up to $10.14$~px) and then progressively returns to its
earlier level by the final laps. This bounded, self-correcting behaviour
indicates the controller does not diverge under sustained operation. Mean
per-lap RMSE still scales with curvature severity ($2.28 < 3.01 < 6.56$~px for
the $45^\circ$, $90^\circ$, and $180^\circ$ courses).
